HALCYON FERRIS -- DVORAK LAYOUT (nils)

Notation:  x/y   = dual function: tap x, hold y
           *Mod  = one-shot modifier (double-tap to lock)
           TO(n) = switch to layer n

==========================================================================
LAYER 0 -- BASE (Dvorak)
==========================================================================

+------+------+------+------+------+   +------+------+------+------+------+
|  '   | , /- | . /! |  p   |  y   |   |  f   |  g   |  c   |  r   |  l   |
+------+------+------+------+------+   +------+------+------+------+------+
|a /Alt|o /Gui|  e   |  u   |  i   |   |  d   |  h   |  t   |n /Gui|s /Alt|
+------+------+------+------+------+   +------+------+------+------+------+
| : /; |  q   |  j   |  k   |  x   |   |  b   |  m   |  w   |  v   |  z   |
+------+------+------+------+------+   +------+------+------+------+------+
                     +------+------+   +------+------+
                     |Sp/Meh| *Sft |   | *Ctl |TO(1) |
                     +------+------+   +------+------+

Home row mods:  a = LAlt   o = LGui   n = RGui   s = LAlt

Umlauts via RAlt (requires EurKey OS layout):
  RAlt + a = ae    RAlt + o = oe    RAlt + u = ue    RAlt + s = ss
